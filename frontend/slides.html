System Architecture & AI Judgment

Two Distinct Layers:
Intelligence + Safety

The Rule: AI decides → Python controls → Razorpay processes
Never trust AI with money
🧠
1. The Cognitive Layer (Gemini)
• Reads complex bank error codes and decline reasons.

• Recommends the right recovery action (e.g., alternate payment for insufficient funds).

• Generates polite, empathetic customer messaging without placing blame.
🔒
2. The Safety Layer (Backend & DB)
• Payment amounts, case statuses, and rules are 100% deterministic.

• Recovery amount is strictly locked to the original failed amount.

• Enforces Stopping Rules: halts on fraud alerts, manual review for GMV > ₹15k.

Failure Recovery & Real Engineering

Problems Faced During Development
and How We Fixed Them

🌐
1. Local Testing & Webhooks
Challenge: Webhooks cannot hit localhost without unstable tunnels.

Fix: Implemented signed webhook verification for production, plus active Razorpay Test API sync for rock-solid local demo reliability.
⚡
2. Idempotency & Safety
Challenge: Rapid clicks could spawn duplicate links or double recovery.

Fix: Enforced state machine checks (PENDING → ACTION_TRIGGERED) and bound Razorpay's reference_id to the case ID.
💰
3. AI Financial Isolation
Challenge: Risk of LLM hallucinating amounts or unauthorized discounts.

Fix: Strictly separated AI recommendations from financial invariants. Python code controls the exact paise sent to Razorpay.
